# 🏈 Load's Draft-o-matic

**Open the app:** https://thefiresays.github.io/draft-board/

To get an icon you can tap, open that link in Chrome, then click the **⋮** menu
in the top right → **Cast, save, and share** → **Install page as app**.

---

## Before draft day — check two settings

Tap **Settings** and make sure these match your league:

- **Starting budget** — set to $100. Many leagues use $200, so change it if yours is different.
- **Roster size** — set to 15 total players. Change it if your league drafts more or fewer.

These two numbers control the **Max bid** number on the main screen, so it's worth
a quick look before you start. You only have to do this once.

---

## How to use it

1. Type a few letters of a player's name.
2. Tap his name in the list.
3. Type the price he sold for.
4. Tap **I won — add to my team**, or **Another team got them**.

That's it. Drafted players disappear from the list so you never pick someone twice.

**The green bar at the top always shows:**

- **Left to spend** — your money remaining
- **Max bid** — the most you can spend on the next player and still fill your roster
- **Roster** — how many players you have

Your draft saves itself. You can close the app and come back to it.

On a laptop, your current roster stays visible beside the player list. On a
phone, use the **My Team** tab as before.

Useful extras:

- Tap **☆ Add to Targets** on a player to build a short watchlist, then use the
  **★ Targets** filter on the draft screen.
- Add a private personal note to any player. Notes save with the draft and its
  backup files.
- When a recent draft-relevant headline is available, the player card shows its
  publisher and date with a link to the source.
- The recent-picks ticker can be hidden from the ticker itself or turned back on
  in **Settings**.

## If something goes wrong

**Wrong player or wrong price?**
Tap **Undo** on the draft screen to reverse the last pick. Tap it again to go back further.

To fix a pick from earlier in the draft, go to **My Team** and tap **Remove** next
to that player. Your money and roster count update right away, and he goes back
into the available list.

**A player isn't in the list?**
Type his name in the search box. When nothing comes up, a box appears — pick his
position, type his team, and tap the add button. He's added for good.

**Want to be safe before draft day?**
The app keeps the latest 12 recovery snapshots automatically, one every five
minutes while the app is open. Restore one from **Settings → Automatic recovery
backups**.

Those snapshots stay on the same device. For an off-device copy, go to
**Settings** → **Save a backup file**. If anything is ever lost, go to
**Settings** → **Restore from a backup file** and pick that file.

**Other settings:**
Budget, roster size, the recent-picks ticker, and downloading your results as a
spreadsheet are all under **Settings**.
